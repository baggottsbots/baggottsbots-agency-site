Political campaigns

Winning Beaufort County campaigns since 2022

In 2022, Beaufort County Sheriff P.J. Tanner tapped Ryan to run the social media for his re-election campaign. Tanner won, and that campaign became the blueprint for WinVotes.ai, Ryan's political marketing platform.

In 2026, WinVotes.ai and BaggottsBOTs built SEO campaign websites, short-form video, paid social and AI follow-up for five more winners: Beaufort County Sheriff, Hilton Head Island Town Council, Beaufort County School Board, and two Beaufort County Council seats. Several were first-time candidates when the work began.
