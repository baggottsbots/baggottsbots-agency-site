Is BaggottsBOTs a digital marketing agency in Bluffton, SC?

Yes. BaggottsBOTs Digital Marketing Agency is based in Bluffton, South Carolina, and serves local and growing businesses in Hilton Head Island, Beaufort, Beaufort County, and the wider Lowcountry. We also work with clients online.

Do I need every service?

Nope. We start with your goals and the biggest gap in your current setup. That might mean better video, one landing page, a chatbot, or a connected campaign, not everything at once.

What happens on the free strategy call?

We'll talk about your business, your customers, and where marketing feels stuck. Then we'll identify useful next steps and whether working together makes sense. No obligation to buy.

Do you offer video production and social media management in Hilton Head Island and Beaufort?

Yes. Video production, editing, and social media management are core services across Bluffton, Hilton Head Island, and Beaufort, SC. We shoot on location in the Lowcountry and handle the editing, posting, and paid promotion.

Will AI replace the human side of my business?

That's not the goal. AI handles repeat questions and routine follow-up so you can spend more time on people. We plan the voice, boundaries, and human handoff around your business.

How much does digital marketing cost in the Lowcountry?

Pricing is custom-scoped around your goals, deliverables, and setup. Paid ad spend is billed separately from management. The strategy call is free, and you receive a clear proposal before committing.
