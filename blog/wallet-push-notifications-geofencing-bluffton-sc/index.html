Wallet push notifications and geofencing in Bluffton, SC: put your brand on their lock screen.

Published November 23, 2026 · By Ryan Baggott · Bluffton, South Carolina

There is a marketing channel hiding inside every phone in Bluffton, and most businesses have no idea it exists. It is the Wallet. The same place your customers keep boarding passes and concert tickets can hold your loyalty card, and it can send a branded notification straight to their lock screen. No app. No inbox. No carrier fee per message.

What is a wallet push notification?

A wallet push notification is a branded message sent to a pass your customer saved in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. It lands on their lock screen like an app notification, but they never had to download an app. Airlines have done this for years for check-in and gate changes. Now a Lowcountry coffee shop, salon or boutique can do the same thing.

Your customers are already drowning. Your email lands under fifty other promos. Your text looks like it came from a random number, and every one of them costs you a carrier fee. A wallet pass sidesteps both problems.

How geofencing fits in

Geofencing draws a virtual boundary around a real place, like your storefront or a busy stretch of town. With wallet passes, your saved store locations let the pass surface on a customer's lock screen when they are nearby, the exact moment a reminder about their reward or today's special is most likely to bring them through the door.

Think about a customer walking down Calhoun Street in Old Town Bluffton or parking at Shelter Cove on Hilton Head Island. Their phone already knows they are close. Your pass can be right there on the lock screen saying "You're 2 visits from a free entree." That is location-based marketing without a single ad dollar.

Frequently asked questions

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. Apple Wallet and Google Wallet come pre-installed on almost every smartphone. Your customer taps one button to add your pass, and your business can reach them from then on.

What is geofencing marketing?

Geofencing marketing draws a virtual boundary around a real place, like your shop or a busy part of town, and uses it to reach people when they are nearby. With wallet passes, your saved store locations let the pass surface on a customer's lock screen when they are close to your business.

Are wallet notifications cheaper than text messages?

Wallet push notifications do not carry a per-message carrier fee the way SMS does, so sending more updates does not mean a bigger texting bill. Setup and management are scoped on a free strategy call.

What can I send to a wallet pass?

Flash sales, new product drops, appointment reminders, event tickets, loyalty points and tier upgrades. The pass itself also updates live, so a customer's points balance or VIP status changes right on the card.

Can wallet passes connect to my existing marketing?

Yes. Wallet pushes are triggered by the same flows, pipelines and AI agents that run your website forms, chat and follow-up, so a new lead, booking or purchase can update a pass or send a notification automatically.

Which businesses in Bluffton and Hilton Head benefit most?

Any business with repeat customers: restaurants, coffee shops, salons and barbershops, boutiques, gyms, golf and marinas, and service companies that book appointments.

The bottom line

Your customers check their lock screen more than any inbox. A wallet pass gives your business a branded, always-there spot on it, with live loyalty status, location-aware reminders and push notifications that cost nothing per send. The channel is wide open. Get there first.
