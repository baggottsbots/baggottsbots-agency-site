What is AI SEO?

AI SEO is search engine optimization done with AI tools helping at each step: researching the questions people ask, drafting and structuring pages, checking titles, descriptions and schema, and submitting pages to Google. The strategy, the facts and the final approval still come from a person who knows the business.

The tools we used were Claude for research, writing and checking, our own platform SuperStack OS to build and publish the pages through its MCP connector, and Muse to handle Google Search Console. MCP is the connection that lets an AI assistant work inside your business software directly, so it can build a page, not just describe one.

The six steps we followed

01Research the real questions

We started on Google, not in a keyword tool. Searching "digital marketing agency Bluffton SC" shows a "People also ask" box with the exact questions locals type:

  • What is the average fee for a digital marketing agency?
  • How much does a digital marketing agency cost?
  • What is the 70/20/10 rule in digital marketing?
  • Which agency is best for digital marketing?

Two of the four are the same question about price. So the first new page was a straight answer: how much a digital marketing agency costs in Bluffton, SC, with our real starting prices in a table.

02Audit what is already there

Next we read our own site the way Google does. The audit found a homepage headline with no keyword in it, page titles that ran too long, share images missing on ten pages, and one services page trying to rank for eight different services at once.

03Build one page per search

Google ranks pages, not sections. So each service got its own page with its own title, a short direct answer near the top, a price table, local details and an FAQ:

  • Website design in Bluffton, SC
  • Social media management
  • Google Ads management
  • Video production
  • Email marketing
  • AI agents and chatbots
  • AI training and workshops
  • Custom software development

04Check every page before it goes live

Fast is only useful if it is right. Before publishing, every one of the 21 pages was downloaded and checked for the basics: a title of 60 characters or fewer, a description of 160 or fewer, exactly one main headline, valid structured data, a share image, and a description on every photo. Problems found in the check were fixed and checked again.

05Tell Google the pages exist

New pages can sit unnoticed for weeks if you wait for Google to find them. We updated the sitemap with all nine new pages, then used Google Search Console to inspect each new address and request indexing. I submitted the first two by hand. Muse, signed in to Search Console, handled the remaining seven.

06Share it where customers already are

The pricing guide went out on Facebook, Instagram and X that same night, in the evening window where our posts get the most clicks, with the link in the first comment.

How long does it take a new page to show up on Google?

A new page can appear on Google within hours or days if you submit your sitemap and request indexing in Google Search Console, but there is no guarantee. Ranking well takes longer, often weeks to months, and depends on your competition, your content, your links and your reviews.

So here is the honest status. The pages are live and Google has been asked to crawl them. Showing up is step one. Ranking near the top for searches like "marketing companies in Bluffton, SC" is earned over the following weeks, and we will update this post with what the data shows.

What AI did not do

  • Set the strategy. Choosing which searches matter came from 17 years of marketing Lowcountry businesses.
  • Invent the facts. Every price on every page is a real price. Nothing was made up to fill a table.
  • Approve its own work. Every page was reviewed on a preview link before it went live.
  • Earn the trust. 4.9 stars from 50+ Google reviews came from clients, one job at a time.

That is the part worth asking any marketing company about. Getting a page to rank is half the job. The page still has to turn a visitor into a call, which is why we build the website, the content, the ads and the follow-up as one system.

Frequently asked questions

Does requesting indexing make a page rank?

No. Requesting indexing only asks Google to crawl the page sooner. Where it ranks depends on how well the page answers the search, how fast and clear the site is, and how much trust the business has earned through reviews, links and consistent listings.

Can AI replace a marketing company?

Not on its own. AI is fast at research, drafting and checking, but it does not know your prices, your customers or what you would never promise. The speed comes from pairing AI with someone who has run local marketing for years and approves every page.

What is the difference between a marketing company and a digital marketing agency?
