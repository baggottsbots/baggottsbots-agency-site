Why Hilton Head SEO is different

Most SEO advice is written for a normal town. Hilton Head Island is not a normal town. You are marketing to two audiences at once, and they search in completely different ways.

  • Residents search like regulars. They want the best, the closest and the most trusted, and they read reviews before they call.
  • Visitors search on the fly. They are on a phone, on a bike path or in a rental kitchen, typing "near me" and choosing from the first three results on the map.
  • Demand is seasonal. A page published the week of the RBC Heritage is too late. The content that wins spring and summer gets published in winter.
  • Neighborhood names matter. People search by Sea Pines, Harbour Town, Coligny, Shelter Cove, Palmetto Dunes, Folly Field and Port Royal Plantation, not just "Hilton Head."

A plan built for Hilton Head covers all four. A generic plan covers none of them.

SEO for Google's AI answers and ChatGPT

AI search optimization means structuring your website, listings and reviews so that tools like Google's AI Overviews, Gemini and ChatGPT can understand your business and recommend it by name when someone asks for a local provider.

Search has changed. Ask Google for "digital marketing Hilton Head" and the first thing many people see is an AI-written answer that names a short list of businesses, then lets you ask follow-up questions like "who is best at what?" When we ran that search in November 2026, BaggottsBOTs was one of three agencies named. That did not happen by accident.

AI answers lean on the same foundations as classic SEO, just read more literally:

  • Direct answers on the page. A clear question followed by a clear two or three sentence answer is easy for AI to quote.
  • Structured data. FAQ, LocalBusiness and service schema spell out who you are, what you offer and where.
  • Consistent listings. The same details everywhere, so the AI is confident it has the right business.
  • Reviews that mention services. When customers describe what you did for them, AI tools pick up what you are known for.

Eight questions to ask any SEO agency

  1. Can you show me your own rankings? An SEO agency that cannot rank itself is a red flag.
  2. What exactly will you do each month? Ask for a list, not a promise.
  3. Who owns the website and the content if I leave? The answer should be you.
  4. Is there a long-term contract? With us, any party can cancel at any time.
  5. How do you handle Google Business Profile? If it is not in the plan, the plan is incomplete.
  6. What are you doing about AI search? A blank stare tells you everything.
  7. How will I know it is working? You should hear calls, leads and bookings, not just "impressions."
  8. Do you guarantee a number one ranking? If they say yes, walk away. Nobody controls Google.
