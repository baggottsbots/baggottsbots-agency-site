Pricing questions

Are these exact prices?

No. They are starting points and typical ranges. Final pricing is scoped on a free strategy call, and you get a clear proposal before any work begins.

Is there a minimum?

Yes. The minimum for any standalone project or engagement is $500.

Is ad spend included?

No. Ad spend is paid to the ad platform and is separate from our fee. Social media plans require at least $500 per month in ad spend plus a 20% agency fee on that spend. On The All-In, ads management is included and the fee drops to 15%.

Are there any other costs?

AI agents and custom software can carry additional AI token costs depending on usage. We review them with you on the strategy call before work starts.

What is the best value?

For a business that wants marketing handled end to end, The All-In starts at $3,997 per month and combines website, social, content, ads management, email and an AI agent. For one specific gap, a single project starting at $500 is usually the better first step.
