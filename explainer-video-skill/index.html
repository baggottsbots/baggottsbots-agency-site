The workflow

How we did it, in 6 simple steps.

No film crew. No editing software. Think of it like covering a song: same structure, brand new lyrics, your band.

01

Pick a video you love

Grab the link to any video whose style and flow you like. Drop it into the app yourself, or hand it to ChatGPT, Grok or Claude and it gets saved to your brand Reference Library, your own cloud storage for every asset.

You do: drop the link or hand it to your AI
02

Let AI watch it and take notes

The AI breaks the video into scenes and writes down what is said, what is shown and how fast it moves.

Result: a scene-by-scene cheat sheet
03

Write your own script

Keep the same story shape (hook, surprise, how it works, call to action) but say it in your words about your business.

Result: a fresh 14-line script
04

Choose the voice and music

Listen to a few voices and pick your favorite. You can even use a clone of your own voice. Then pick a music vibe.

Result: voiceover and a beat
05

Build the scenes with your brand

Your logo, your colors, real app icons and real QR codes. Nothing fake, no gray placeholder boxes.

Result: a branded video
06

Add captions, check and share

Captions pop up exactly when each word is spoken. We check every key moment, then post it on your channels.

Result: a ready-to-post video
Why this works: the hard part of a great explainer is the structure, and someone already figured that out. You borrow the structure, then make everything else yours.
