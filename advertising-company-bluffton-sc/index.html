How much should a Bluffton business spend on advertising?

A workable starting ad budget for a local Bluffton business is $500 per month on one platform. That is enough to test audiences and messages in a town this size. Budgets go up when the numbers show the ads are paying for themselves.

  • Start with one platform. Splitting a small budget across four places teaches you nothing.
  • Send clicks somewhere good. An ad is only as strong as the page it lands on. If your site is slow or unclear, fix that first with website design.
  • Answer every lead. Paying for a lead and missing the call is the most expensive mistake in local advertising. An AI agent can answer at any hour.

Which is better for a Bluffton business, Google Ads or Facebook ads?

It depends on how people buy from you. Google Ads reaches people who are already searching for your service, which suits home services and professionals.
