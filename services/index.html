Pricing questions

Straight answers.

Still not sure which service fits? Send us a note or book the free call.

Are the prices on this page exact quotes?

No. Every price is a starting point or a typical range. Final pricing is scoped per client on the free strategy call.

What is the minimum to work with BaggottsBOTs?

$500 is our bare minimum for any standalone project or service engagement. The only lines under $500 are the self-serve SuperStack OS subscription, which is a product with no agency labor, and add-ons that attach to a core service.

Is ad spend included in social media management?

No. Ad spend is required and billed separately. The minimum is $500 per month on every tier, plus a 20% agency fee on that spend. We may recommend more as you grow, but $500 per month is all we require. On The All-In, ads management is included and the agency fee drops to 15%.

Can I manage my own website?

Yes. You can self-manage on SuperStack OS, our AI Business OS, for $79 per month with $0 setup if you do it yourself, or a $499 one-time done-for-you setup. Two AI agents are included, and you can manage the site in the UI or through ChatGPT, Grok, or Claude via MCP.

What is The All-In plan?

The All-In is the plan we recommend, starting from $3,997 per month. We handle your website, blogging and SEO, top-level social media, a monthly content shoot, email, a managed omnichannel AI agent, and ads management. You get a customized, fully branded dashboard for real-time reporting, and you can use ChatGPT, Claude, or Grok to get instant insights or take other actions. It has a 3-month minimum, and the $999 setup is waived on 6 months.

Are there any additional costs?

Additional AI token costs may apply, and ad spend is always separate from management fees. We cover both on your strategy call before anything starts.
